How this prototype checks

A transparent demonstration, not an AI diagnosis or a validated interaction engine.

Medicine reference lookup

Recognises selected medicine names listed in your uploaded two-page pharmaceutical guide and displays the guide's example strengths and stated uses.

Completeness checks

Highlights blank medicine names or missing details so the information can be confirmed by a clinician or pharmacist.

Duplicate-name check

Flags repeated entered medicine names for human review. It does not prove that ingredients, doses, or formulations are equivalent.

Not included

No validated drug–drug or drug–food interaction database, dose appropriateness engine, kidney/liver function checks, pregnancy checks, allergy verification, or personalised clinical rules.

Reference source: Top Prescribed Pharmaceuticals Guide (uploaded PDF)It lists selected active ingredients, example available strengths and primary uses. It states that actual dosages must be individualised by licensed healthcare professionals. It is not sufficient to assess an individual's prescription safety.
Development principle

The language model should explain only verified findings. It must not invent interaction alerts or turn missing information into a claim that a medicine is safe.
